Delivery & help

How ordering works, what delivery and collection look like, and answers to the questions we get asked most.

How to order

Three steps

Fill your bag

Browse the shop and add what you want. Everything listed is in stock.

Send it on WhatsApp

Open your bag, choose collect or deliver, and tap send. Your order arrives as a message to us.

We confirm, you pay

We check everything is packed, confirm the total (and delivery cost) and send you payment details. Then it's yours.

Getting it to you

Ways to get it

Collect from a store

Pick it up at any of our 8 stores once we've told you it's ready.

  • No delivery fee
  • Bring your ID
  • Choose your store in your bag

Courier across SA

We deliver anywhere in South Africa, usually in 2 to 3 working days. Same-day delivery is possible in selected areas near our stores.

  • Free on orders over R1,500
  • ID checked on delivery (18+)
  • Updates on WhatsApp until it arrives

Tonight with 7Waves

Near our Germiston store in Primrose? 7Waves riders deliver from our counter until closing time, usually in 30 to 40 minutes. Launching end of November 2026.

  • Choose "7Waves" in your bag
  • Copy your list in one tap
  • Open 7Waves and order
FAQs

Good questions

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp or call +27 61 040 0252.

Do you deliver outside Gauteng?

Yes, anywhere in South Africa. Delivery is free on orders over R1,500. Under that, we'll confirm the cost on WhatsApp before you pay.

What is 7Waves?

7Waves is a delivery service built only for vape shops, starting with our Germiston store in Primrose. You pay shop prices plus a delivery fee that goes to the rider. The rider checks your ID (18+) and a 4-digit delivery code before handing over your order.

How long does delivery take?

Courier orders usually take 2 to 3 working days. Same-day delivery is available in selected areas near our stores. 7Waves orders from Primrose usually arrive in 30 to 40 minutes.

Can I order online and collect in a store?

Yes. Choose "Collect" in your bag and pick your store. We'll let you know on WhatsApp when it's packed and waiting.

How do I pay?

Online payments go through Bob Pay: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Instant EFT, Capitec Pay, Scan to Pay, Absa Pay, Apple Pay and Google Pay. You can also pay in 4 interest-free instalments with Payflex. Order on WhatsApp and we'll send you a payment link once your order is confirmed.

Are your products genuine?

Yes. 100% genuine, no clones. We buy from the brands and their official distributors.

Is everything on the website really in stock?

The website only lists products that are in stock on our stock list. Things do sell fast, so if something goes before we confirm your order, we'll tell you straight away and suggest something similar.

Do I need to be 18?

Yes. We only sell to adults and check ID in the shop, on collection and on delivery.

Which coil or pod fits my device?

Use our Coil & Pod Finder: pick your device and it shows every coil, pod and tank that fits, in stock now. Still not sure? Send us a photo of your device on WhatsApp.

My device stopped working. What now?

Message us with your order details and what's happening, and we'll help you sort it out.

Do you sell wholesale?

Yes. We supply vape shops, convenience stores, garages and tuck shops from Germiston. Email sales@volcanovapes.co.za or call 072 425 9588.

Can you get a product you don't have?

Often, yes. Tell us what you're after and we'll check with our suppliers.
