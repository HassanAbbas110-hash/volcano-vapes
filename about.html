Who we are

Started in 2022

Volcano Vapes started in 2022. Today we run eight stores: two in Potchefstroom, and one each in Germiston, Pretoria, Delmas, Klerksdorp, Ballito and Gqeberha.

Everything ships from our distribution centre in Germiston, which also supplies other shops: vape shops, convenience stores, garages and tuck shops.

We keep the shelves stacked with the newest drops, and everything is the real thing from the brands themselves. Not sure what to get? Message us on WhatsApp and a real person will help.
